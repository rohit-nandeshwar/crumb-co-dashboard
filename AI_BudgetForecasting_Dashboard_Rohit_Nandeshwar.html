History: 36 simulated months (Nov 2023–Oct 2026). Forecast: next 12 months. The shaded band is an indicative 80% range based on the back-test error. Scenario sliders move the forecast lines.
Models were fitted on Nov 2023–Oct 2025 only and asked to predict the next 12 months, which were held back as “actual” months.

MAE = mean absolute error; MAPE = mean absolute percentage error; bias = forecast minus actual (negative = under-forecast).

  • Business. Crumb & Co.: hypothetical neighbourhood bakery-café; variable costs about 42% of sales (materials 28%, labour 8%, overhead 6%); fixed costs Rs 170k, 175k and 180k a month over three years.
  • Data. 36 simulated months (seed 2026): base Rs 420k growing 0.9% a month, seasonal index 0.90–1.22, 3.5% noise. Not records of a real business.
  • Traditional. Least-squares trend on sales; variable costs at the historical ratio; fixed costs by assumption; CVP break-even = fixed costs ÷ contribution margin ratio.
  • AI predictive. Holt-Winters exponential smoothing (damped additive trend, multiplicative 12-month seasonality) for sales; cost-behaviour regression of variable cost on sales for expenses.
  • Scenarios. The sliders apply a percentage change to the model sales forecast, change next year’s fixed-cost growth, and shift the variable-cost ratio in percentage points. They are what-if tools, not new forecasts.
  • Caveats. Simulated data with designed seasonality; one back-test window; the seasonal-naive reference (last year’s month times last year’s growth) was more accurate than both methods in the back-test.
